# EduPrep Phase 1 Design System

## Direction
Modern, clean, academic, encouraging, trustworthy and uncluttered.

## Platform
Mobile-first, especially Android and smaller screens.

## Theme
Centralise Flutter theme tokens:
primary, secondary, success, error, warning, info, surface, background, textPrimary, textSecondary, border.

Do not hardcode colours in individual widgets.

## Typography
Readable sans-serif with display, headline, title, body, bodySmall and caption levels. Question text should be prominent.

## Spacing
8-point system: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48.

## Components
App bar, buttons, subject cards, topic cards, question card, answer option, progress indicator, result summary, loading, empty and error states.

## Answer states
Default, Selected, Correct, Incorrect, Disabled. Do not rely on colour alone; use text/icons.

## Practice UX
Keep the question screen focused and minimise unnecessary navigation.

## Errors
Use plain-language messages rather than raw technical errors.

## AI
Not implemented in Phase 1. Reserve space for future learning assistance without building a chatbot.
